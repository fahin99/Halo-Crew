HALOCREW VISIONGUARD — MODULAR ASTRONAUT EYE-HEALTH CAD CONCEPT
Native FreeCAD project | millimeters | generation verified in FreeCAD 1.1.4

OPEN FIRST
HaloCrew_VisionGuard_Complete.FCStd is the actual native project. It contains
separate solid components, nested subsystem groups, editable manufactured-part
sketches, pads/pockets, native box/cylinder dimensions, and identification data.
Rotate freely with FreeCAD's navigation controls. Press 0 for isometric; V,F
fits the complete assembly. Use the included Configurations macro to select
assembled, exploded, transparent casing, sensor arrows, six standard views,
or a timed camera orbit. The configuration menu modifies the active document;
close without saving to return to the delivered assembled view.

ARCHITECTURE AND PACKAGING
A. Wearable glasses: two inward IR cameras, eight recessed IR-emitter packages,
   pupil/blink measurements sharing those cameras, an IMU, processor PCB,
   microdisplay and concept coupler, ambient-light/temperature/humidity sensors,
   antenna, protected battery envelope, power PCB and charging contacts;
   two clear lenses, silicone pads, hinges, detachable temple segments, FPC
   trace routing and serviceable pods. Lens apertures are 53 x 37 mm; bridge
   aperture spacing 19 mm; nominal temples 145 mm. The electronics increase
   external width to approximately 150 mm. This is a prototype packaging
   concept, not ordinary slim prescription eyewear or an EVA pressure garment.
B. Separate supported instruments:
   OCT concept enclosure: 150 x 190 x 95 mm before ports, cover and mount.
   Models optical bench, source/cooling package, reference collimator/mirror,
   splitter, galvo scanner packages, objective/lenses, focus screw, fixation
   target, hollow spectrometer shell, grating, focus optic, line-array detector,
   controller, cabling and illustrative optical paths.
   Fundus enclosure: 100 x 155 x 85 mm before objective and mount. Models
   objective/relay optics, annular illuminators, splitter, stop, image sensor,
   alignment camera, focus rail and electronics.
   Manual refractive test: three exchangeable test optics, target display,
   alignment ring, rails, adjustment screw and receiver. No autorefractor claim.
   Modules share a 36 mm rail-center spacing concept with release latch and
   spring contacts. A separate examination stand supports their weight.
   Glasses locating hardware aligns a frame; glasses do not support OCT mass.
C. Separate instruments: ocular ultrasound probe/transducer, console with
   electronics/battery/display, routed cable and cradle; rebound-tonometer
   probe, contact tip, guide/coil, electronics, battery, controls, alignment pad
   and protective case; visual display station with Amsler target geometry,
   occluder, chin/forehead supports, height-adjustable posts and 500 mm nominal
   alignment-distance rails. Rail/frame material is a packaging illustration.

EDITING AND PARAMETRIC SCOPE
Expand VisionGuard System > Design Parameters. LensOpening, LensHeight and
FrameThickness drive fully constrained chamfered rim sketches/pads. Housing
parameters such as OCT_ConceptHousingEnvelopeWidth/Depth/Height drive native
housing pad/pocket dimensions. Major native box and cylinder components expose
editable Length/Width/Height/Radius properties. Main housings include modeled
objective ports and ventilation pockets. Sketches and individual dimensions
remain inspectable in their PartDesign bodies.
Detailed rings, lens plates, fasteners, cable routing and some mechanical
features use independent Part solids generated by recipes in the macro. Their
placement and identification remain editable; changing their detailed geometry
requires editing the recipe or normal FreeCAD modeling operations. This is a
mixed parametric CAD concept, not an entirely constraint-solved production
assembly. Resizing an enclosure does not automatically reposition every PCB,
optic or screw. Recheck optical alignment and interference after changes.
The general design values on Design Parameters document nominal envelopes;
the named housing parameters and native features are the geometry drivers.

REGENERATING
Use Macro > Macros in a FreeCAD GUI environment and execute the Generator.
Outputs are placed beside the macro when FreeCAD supplies __file__; otherwise
in ~/VisionGuard. VISIONGUARD_OUTPUT can explicitly specify the output folder.
The generator creates the native file, STEP, CSV, tessellation, validation
report and standard CAD PNGs. It reports errors through the FreeCAD console.
Regeneration replaces outputs of the same name and creates a fresh document.
Save your current work before rerunning.
To rebuild the supplementary numbered sheets, execute VisionGuard_Inspection
in the same output folder. This reopens and validates the native project,
checks parameter changes and housing interference, then captures actual CAD
views and projection coordinates. It does not save inspection poses over the
native file. Reopen the original afterward for its normal saved view.
Finally run: python VisionGuard_Presentation_Exporter.py
Dependencies for that supplementary exporter: Python, NumPy and Pillow. It
creates GLB and the polished numbered PNG sheets from the actual tessellation
and camera-projected anchors. No network or image-generation service is used.

CLINICAL WORKFLOW — SCREENING IS DISTINCT FROM DIAGNOSIS
Eye tracking + IMU + environmental context -> continuous eye metrics ->
personal baseline comparison -> anomaly flag -> HaloCrew dashboard.
These CAD files do not implement signal-processing algorithms or a dashboard.
Pupil/blink/eye movement data alone do not diagnose SANS, establish optic-disc
edema, measure retinal layer thickness, or prove elevated intracranial pressure.
Scheduled OCT + fundus + ocular ultrasound + tonometry + visual assessment ->
structural and functional measurements -> SANS-related medical evaluation ->
qualified medical review. A full comprehensive exam and any indicated MRI are
external clinical services; an MRI system is outside this modeled package.
Visual field targets here are exploratory; the flat near-distance screen is
not a validated perimeter. The station is calibration-ready geometry, not a
calibrated delivered test system. Its 500 mm distance is appropriate only for
suitably calibrated near-test protocols, not a universal distance acuity test.

LIMITATIONS AND REMAINING ENGINEERING
Geometric completeness is not functional completeness. No lens prescriptions,
ray trace, OCT dispersion/path-length budget, scan calibration, source power,
IEC exposure assessment, acoustic-output validation, tonometer force/accuracy,
electrical schematic, firmware, EMC, battery qualification, thermal simulation,
biocompatibility, sterilization validation or spaceflight qualification is
provided. IR-emitter packages are recessed, but their geometry does not prove
eye safety. Display luminance/contrast/size need calibration. The listed test
optics have no assigned or verified dioptric power. Materials/colors are visual
categories, not supplier specifications. No NASA approval or affiliation is
claimed. Exploded offsets show identification, not a validated service order.
Contact/joint fastener intersections and mechanical mating are intentional;
this is not a certified collision-free or manufacture-ready design.
